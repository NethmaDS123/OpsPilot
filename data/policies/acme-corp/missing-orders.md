# Missing or Stolen Packages

## Definition

An order is "missing" when tracking shows **delivered** but the customer did not receive it, or when tracking ends without delivery scan for **14+ days**.

## Customer steps

1. Check with household members and building management within **24 hours** of marked delivery.
2. File a police report for suspected porch theft when value exceeds **$150** (required for replacement).
3. Contact Acme support with order ID and any carrier photos.

## Acme investigation

- We query the carrier within **2 business days**.
- Signature-required deliveries without signature waive Acme liability unless carrier error is proven.
- First-time missing-package reports under $150 may receive **one courtesy replacement** per account per 12 months.

## Outcomes

- **Confirmed loss**: Replacement or refund at customer choice.
- **Delivered to wrong address**: Acme arranges intercept or refund after carrier confirmation.
- **Fraud suspicion**: Account flagged; no replacement until investigation completes.

## High-value orders

Orders over **$1,000** require signature or hub pickup; "delivered" without signature triggers automatic Tier 2 review.
