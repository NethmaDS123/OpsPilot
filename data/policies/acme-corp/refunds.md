# Customer Refund Policy

## Overview

Acme Corp processes refunds for eligible purchases made through acmecorp.com or the Acme mobile app. This policy defines eligibility windows, partial refund rules, and processing timelines.

## Eligibility

1. **Standard products**: Full refund within **30 days** of delivery if the item is unused and in original packaging.
2. **Digital goods**: Refund within **14 days** only if the license key has not been activated.
3. **Subscription services**: Pro-rated refunds are available within **7 days** of renewal if no premium features were consumed.
4. **Final sale items**: Marked "Final Sale" at checkout are **not refundable** unless required by local law.

## Partial refunds

- Opened but defective items may receive a **50% refund** if the customer declines replacement.
- Bundles: If one component is kept, refund is calculated on the remaining returnable line items only.
- Shipping fees are refunded only when Acme caused the error (wrong item shipped, duplicate charge).

## Processing

- Refunds return to the **original payment method** within **5–10 business days** after warehouse inspection.
- Store credit may be offered for faster resolution; customer must opt in explicitly.
- Refunds above **$500** require supervisor approval and identity verification.

## Exclusions

Refunds are denied when:
- The return window has expired without a documented Acme shipping delay.
- Evidence of fraud, chargeback abuse, or repeated serial returns is present.
- Items show signs of misuse inconsistent with the reported defect.

## Escalation

Customers disputing a refund denial may request a **Tier 2 review** within 14 days by referencing order ID and prior case number.
