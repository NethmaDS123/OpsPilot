# Shipping and Delivery Delays

## Standard delivery promises

- Domestic standard: **3–7 business days** after fulfillment.
- Express: **1–2 business days** in contiguous US.
- International: **10–21 business days** depending on customs.

Delivery dates shown at checkout are estimates, not guarantees, unless **Guaranteed Delivery** was purchased.

## When a package is late

1. If tracking shows no movement for **72 hours**, open a delay ticket.
2. After **5 business days** past the estimated delivery date, customers may request a **$10 courtesy credit** (once per order).
3. After **10 business days**, customers may cancel for a full refund if the item has not shipped, or choose replacement/refund if lost in transit.

## Carrier investigations

Investigations begin when tracking stagnates for 7 days or the carrier marks "exception." Acme does not refund while an active investigation is open unless the carrier declares loss.

## Weather and force majeure

During declared emergencies, delay credits are discretionary; refund windows for returns extend by the number of delay days documented on the order.

## Address errors

Delays caused by customer address typos are not eligible for courtesy credits; re-ship fees may apply.
