# Account Access and Security

## Password reset

- Self-service reset via verified email or SMS; links expire in **15 minutes**.
- After **3 failed logins**, the account locks for **30 minutes**.
- Agents **never** send passwords in plain text or reset credentials without multi-factor verification.

## Account recovery

If email access is lost, customers must provide:
- Government ID matching the account name, and
- Last four digits of a payment method used in the past 90 days.

Recovery completes within **24–48 hours** after document review.

## Suspicious activity

Signs include new shipping addresses, bulk orders, or logins from new countries. Acme may temporarily freeze purchases and require password change plus MFA enrollment.

## Authorized users

Business accounts may add delegates with **view-only** or **purchase** roles; delegates cannot change billing or delete the primary account.

## Data access

Customers may export personal data once per month. Agents cannot disclose another customer's order history or payment details.

## Prohibited support actions

Support staff must not:
- Disable MFA at customer request without identity proof.
- Merge accounts across different email addresses without legal documentation.
