# Order and Subscription Cancellations

## Pre-shipment orders

Customers may cancel any order that has **not entered carrier possession**. Cancellation is immediate and payment authorization is released within **3 business days**.

## In-transit cancellations

Once shipped, cancellation is not available; customers must refuse delivery or use the Returns Policy after receipt.

## Subscriptions

- **Monthly**: Cancel anytime; access continues until period end. No refund for partial months unless required by law.
- **Annual**: Cancel within **14 days** of purchase for full refund if no premium consumption occurred; otherwise pro-rated refund minus **$25 admin fee**.
- **Trial**: Cancel before trial end to avoid charge; reminders sent at day 5 and day 12 of a 14-day trial.

## Backorders

Customers may cancel backorders without penalty at any time before shipment notification.

## Bundle cancellations

Cancelling one item in a promotional bundle voids bundle pricing; remaining items are re-priced at standard rates before cancellation finalizes.

## Agent authority

Tier 1 agents may cancel orders under $200. Higher values require supervisor token.
