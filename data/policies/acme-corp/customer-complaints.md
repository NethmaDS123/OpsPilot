# Customer Complaints and Escalations

## Filing a complaint

Customers may file complaints via support chat, email, or web form. Each receives a **case ID** within **4 business hours**.

## Severity levels

- **Level 1**: Service quality, delays, minor product issues—target resolution **48 hours**.
- **Level 2**: Repeated failures, financial harm under $500—target **5 business days**.
- **Level 3**: Safety, discrimination, regulatory concerns—immediate human review, target **24 hours**.

## Remedies

Depending on severity, remedies include apology, courtesy credits, expedited shipping on next order, or policy exception review. Monetary compensation above **$100** requires manager approval.

## Escalation path

1. Frontline agent
2. Team lead
3. Customer relations manager
4. External ombudsman (EU/UK customers after 30 days without resolution)

## Recording and privacy

Calls may be recorded for quality; customers may opt out except where legally required for dispute resolution.

## Abusive conduct

Acme may restrict service for threats toward staff; complaints still processed through written channel.

## No retaliation

Customers exercising complaint rights are not denied standard policy benefits in retaliation.
