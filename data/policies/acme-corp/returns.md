# Product Returns and Exchanges

## Return window

Physical goods may be returned within **45 days** of delivery. Extended holiday returns (until January 31) apply to purchases made between November 1 and December 24.

## Condition requirements

- Items must include all accessories, manuals, and original packaging.
- Hygiene-sealed products cannot be returned if the seal is broken (exceptions for documented defects).
- Personalized or engraved items are **exchange-only** within 14 days for manufacturing errors.

## Return shipping

- Acme provides a **prepaid label** for defects, wrong items, or damaged-in-transit claims.
- Customer pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns unless they hold **Acme Plus** membership.
- Drop-off at authorized carriers; no refund until the package is scanned at the hub.

## Exchanges

- Size/color exchanges are free within the return window if stock is available.
- Price differences are charged or refunded at the time of exchange approval.
- Cross-category exchanges require manager approval.

## Restocking fee

A **15% restocking fee** applies to opened electronics over $200 returned without defect.

## Refund linkage

Approved returns automatically trigger the [Refund Policy](acme-refunds-v2) workflow once inspection completes.
