# Globex Refund Standards

Globex Inc. offers refunds within **14 days** for unopened merchandise only.

All refunds are issued as **Globex store credit**; cash refunds require director approval.

Digital products are **non-refundable** after download.

This policy applies **only** to globex-inc customers and orders prefixed `GX-`.
