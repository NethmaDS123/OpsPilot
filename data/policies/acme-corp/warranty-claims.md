# Manufacturer and Extended Warranty Claims

## Standard manufacturer warranty

Acme honors manufacturer warranties shipped with the product. Typical coverage:
- Electronics: **1 year** parts and labor.
- Small appliances: **2 years**.
- Outdoor power equipment: **3 years** limited.

Proof of purchase from Acme satisfies warranty registration.

## Acme Care extended plans

Optional plans add:
- Accidental damage (2 claims per year, **$50 deductible** each).
- Priority repair queue.
- Loaner devices for laptops and phones when repair exceeds **5 business days**.

## Claim process

1. Submit serial number, purchase date, and fault description.
2. Remote diagnostics may be required before RMA issuance.
3. Customer ships device using Acme-provided label for covered failures.

## Exclusions

Warranty does not cover cosmetic damage, unauthorized modifications, commercial misuse, or damage from improper storage.

## Lemon policy

If the same defect is repaired **three times** within 12 months, customer may elect replacement or refund of purchase price.

## Third-party marketplace

Items marked "Fulfilled by Acme" on partner marketplaces follow this policy; third-party-only sellers refer to their own warranty.
