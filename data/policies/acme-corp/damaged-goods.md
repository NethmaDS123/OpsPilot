# Damaged or Defective Goods

## Reporting timeline

Customers must report visible shipping damage within **48 hours** of delivery with photos of the outer box, inner packaging, and product.

Manufacturing defects may be reported within **one year** for most categories, or **two years** for Acme Pro appliances.

## Evidence requirements

Required: order number, photos, and description of damage. Optional but helpful: video unboxing.

Without photos within 48 hours, transit damage claims may be limited to **store credit**.

## Resolution options

1. **Replacement**: Shipped at no cost when inventory allows (priority for Plus members).
2. **Repair**: Authorized service for items over $300 when replacement stock is zero.
3. **Refund**: Full refund including shipping if replacement is declined or unavailable within 10 business days.

## Concealed damage

If damage is discovered after 48 hours but within 7 days, Tier 2 may approve a claim with carrier investigation.

## Safety recalls

If a product is under recall, customers receive automatic notification; refunds or replacements are mandatory regardless of purchase date.
